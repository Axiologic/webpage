Axiologic Research Editions

Books we wanted to read, but could not find.

These are human-directed, AI-assisted books and literary experiments.

They explore Outfinitism and meta-rationality.

They also explore intelligence, freedom, power and social technologies.

The collection also presents our technologies.

It shows how we test them and what our research finds.

Human authors retain the thesis and direction.

They also retain selection and final editorial responsibility.

AI is an instrument for research, drafting, criticism and exploration.

These books are part of a research project on high-quality literature.

They are made with AI assistance and human judgment.

They are free to read.

More readers, critics and collaborators can engage with them.

We love books because some problems are complex.

They require a patient, step-by-step reading.

Without that space, understanding does not have time to happen.

A book can make an unfamiliar idea click into place.

It gives that idea more space than the speed of an opinion.

Alan Kay is often credited with saying this:

“A change of perspective is worth 80 IQ points.”

AI can produce slop, but it can also accelerate the explanation of complex ideas.

Credible content can now be produced quickly.

Validation, selection and discovery remain scarce.

Our research programme makes valuable work easier to examine, compare and correct.

It also helps bring that work forward responsibly.

None of these books is a one-shot output.

A single book may pass through dozens of versions.

It may be re-read, challenged and revised.

We return to the text until an original idea becomes clear.

It should be able to stand on its own.

The books are not yet perfect, but some may still prove useful.

Feedback and improved verification tools will help us publish better editions.

Many of these books draw inspiration from Outfinitism.

It is an original philosophy developed by Axiologic founder Sînică Alboaie.

It makes meta-rationality more concrete.

It responds to the dangers of a technologically advanced world.

Many books are also available in French, German, Spanish and Portuguese.

They are also available in Italian, Romanian and Polish.

Where available, use the language menu to read a translation online.

You can also download its PDF edition.
