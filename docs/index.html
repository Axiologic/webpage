BOOKSA book begins with a question. It grows through attention, revision, evidence and the patient work of making an idea clear. The page holds what thought discovers before it becomes a system. Intelligence changes the shape of work, but every system begins with a question. Every question needs a careful frame, a source, a reader and a reason to continue. Evidence, language, action and consequence become part of the same page. A book is a place where ideas can remain open. 01   A FIELD OF POSSIBILITIES

We make maps before we build roads. The work is to keep each decision visible, each claim open to revision, and every horizon large enough for a new question. There is no useful intelligence without the patience to name its limits.A book is not a machine for closing a question. It is a room where ideas can be tested, revised and carried farther by another reader. Each page holds a small invitation to continue. 02   THE HUMAN THREAD

Tools become useful when they return attention to the people who use them. A system should enlarge judgment, not replace the need for it. The work continues wherever a person can still decide what matters.The human thread is not a missing feature. It is the reason tools have a direction at all. Every reliable system needs an author, a reader and the freedom to ask again. 03   NOTES TOWARD TOMORROW

A system should explain what it knows, what it assumes, and what it cannot know yet. That is where trust begins. The future is made from the questions we leave possible.Tomorrow is not a final page. It is a draft made visible through care, argument and imagination. What matters is not only what a system can do, but what it permits us to become.

Axiologic Research

ADVANCED TECHNOLOGIES for TRUSTWORTHY AI and INTELLIGENT AGENTS.

Axiologic Research is an AI lab and software development company founded in 2003 by Sînică Alboaie. It has grown through diverse consulting activities and European research projects. Since 2020, we have built strong industrial links through the PharmaLedger research initiative, and we have also worked independently with major pharmaceutical companies: Novartis, GSK, MSD, Roche, UCB, Takeda.

Across dozens of commercial and research projects delivered since 2003, we have built an extensive body of innovations and research insights in enterprise software, compilers, privacy-preserving technologies, blockchain, including the OpenDSU ecosystem for enterprise blockchain, mobile applications, cloud and DevOps, and advanced AI research.

Since 2024, we have been contributing to ACHILLES, a Horizon Europe research project advancing trustworthy AI and intelligent agents. The European Commission's Innovation Radar has recognised Axiologic as a Key Innovator behind two market-ready innovations with high market-creation potential.

From 2026 onward, the validation, market adoption, and venture development of future projects will be carried forward through Outfinity Venture Validation Studio in Switzerland. Outfinity connects our research outcomes with industry partners and credible paths to deployment, while the AssistOS open-source ecosystem makes the underlying technologies available to communities and integrators. Building on the two recognised innovations, AssistOS expresses our ambition to deepen the understanding of AI agents, automate scientific research and reliable long-form analysis for highly regulated industries, and advance human and AI collaboration through an open-source environment designed for the safe, coherent, and legally aligned deployment and use of AI agents.

If you want to understand how we think, the direction of our research and the technologies we build, begin with one of our more than 50 free books. Created with AI assistance and used to test trustworthy-AI and validation technologies, they remain human-directed works built around original ideas, some deliberately experimental, others more established, all selected for their distinctive and useful perspectives.
