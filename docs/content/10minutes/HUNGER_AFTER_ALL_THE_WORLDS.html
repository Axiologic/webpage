036. COMPOUND-01

Formula: Alternative Fundamental Constants + Psychohistory + Cognitive Language • Genealogy: original synthesis of the Table This compound couples Alternative Fundamental Constants with Psychohistory, then adds Cognitive Language. The first premise says that different constants produce different chemistry and different stars. The second premise says that populations are predicted mathematically. The third premise says that language changes the space of thought. The resulting world is not defined by the sum of its gadgets but by coupled consequences: who controls the new capacity, what becomes scarce, what kind of person appears, and which old institution can no longer function unchanged. In the periodic laboratory, a compound counts as stable only if it produces at least one consequence that is not obvious from each element separately. That is the difference between decorative combinatorics and speculative generation: interaction must create a new problem, not merely a longer inventory.

The Propagation Rule

To design a new world with the Table, do not begin with the number of elements. Choose one and propagate it. Alternative gravity must reach the skeleton, architecture, sport, war, religion, and language. Uploading must reach inheritance, punishment, marriage, insurance, and mourning. Contract magic must reach the legal profession, the education of children, and the way a society defines consent.
