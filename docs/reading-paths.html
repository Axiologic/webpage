Reading paths

Follow an argument, not a shelf.

Each route begins with a clear entry point and opens toward a neighbouring question. The arrows are invitations, not a required order.

Power & Civilisation

A route through the social systems that hide costs, distribute responsibility and choose what they hold sacred.

Care & Freedom

Stories and arguments about protection, refusal and the danger of making benevolence immune to criticism.

Language & Evidence

Start where a museum turns harm into a label, move through the zodiac’s fair trial, then ask how a civilisation decides which explanations deserve to survive contact with evidence.

SF: Second Selves

Begin with the refusal of a continuity shell, then follow the social consequences of copies, memory and lives that may be continued past consent.

SF: Care Systems

These worlds begin with protection—medical systems, abundance and social coordination—and make the reader feel the moment at which help may become rule.

SF: Distant Worlds

Travel outward through Aethon, the Cascade and a catastrophic report: alien perspectives turn violence, creation and historical guilt into problems too large for one protagonist.

Human–AI Experiments

Books that turn AI into an interlocutor, critical constraint or research instrument rather than an invisible substitute author.

Executable Science

Move from language that sounds plausible to claims whose sources, tests and corrections can remain visible.

Agentic AI

From the operating layer to bounded delegation: how intelligence becomes useful without becoming unaccountable.
